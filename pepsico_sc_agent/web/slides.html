Technical Architecture (MOP-SC-042)

Algorithmic Formulas & Logic

1. Days of Supply ($\text{DOS}$)
DOS = Current On-Hand Inventory / Daily Sales Run Rate

Evaluated against the 7.0-day Safety Stock Threshold. Any SKU under 3.0 days is escalated to Critical.

2. SLA-Weighted Revenue Risk Index
Risk = Deficit × (∑ Share_i × Weight_i) × (Rev / 1000)

Tier 1 (Walmart, Target): 2.5× weight + OTIF chargeback risk.
Tier 2 (Regional Grocery): 1.5× weight | Tier 3: 1.0×

3. Expedited Freight Linehaul Model
Linehaul Cost = (Highway Miles × $4.20/mile × FTLs) + (Pallets × $15.00 Handling)

Uses team-driver expedited transit speeds (48 mph effective DOT) to compute exact recovery lead times in hours.
